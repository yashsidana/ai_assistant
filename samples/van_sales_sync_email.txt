Subject: Requirements for Van Sales Direct Store Delivery (DSD) Module

Hi Arjun,

Following our kickoff discussion, here are the core requirements for the Van Sales module for our beverage distribution fleet:

1. Van Inventory Tracking: Each morning, the van driver must acknowledge the loaded crate count in the handheld app before beginning the route.
2. Spot Billing & Cash Collection: During each store visit, the van rep must generate an invoice on the spot and print a receipt via Bluetooth thermal printer. The payment mode (cash, UPI, or credit) must be recorded.
3. End-of-Day Settlement: The app must reconcile remaining van inventory against total sales and cash collected. The reconciliation process should be seamless and easy for drivers.
4. Offline Support: Drivers operate in basements and rural highways. Spot invoicing and receipt generation must work 100% offline.
5. ERP Integration: When returning to the depot, end-of-day settlement numbers must sync to SAP S/4HANA.
